# Hairpin

A top-down pixel art time-trial race: three laps as fast as you can, against the ghost of your best time.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Controls

- **↑**: accelerate, **↓**: brake.
- **← →**: steer.
- **Enter**: restart at once (even mid-race).
- **Space**: start. **P**: pause.

## How it works

- **Six tracks**: Meadow, Hairpins, Ice Field, Dunes, Turbo and Knot.
- **Asphalt** grips. **Grass** and **sand** slow you down, **ice** slides, **blue arrows** give a turbo boost.
- Four **blue posts** mark each lap. At each one, the **gap** to your best time shows in green (faster) or red.
- Your best time drives next to you as a **ghost**.
- **Bronze**, **silver**, **gold** and **author** medals. A medal unlocks the next track.

## Stats

The Bests panel keeps, for each track, the **best time**, the **best lap**, the **medal** and the time to beat for gold.

## Data and sync

Your times and ghosts are saved by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs if sync is on.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Hairpin → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
